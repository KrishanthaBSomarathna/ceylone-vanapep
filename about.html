ABOUT US

Rooted in Sri Lanka. Trusted by the World.

Ceylone VanaPep (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan company dedicated to exporting the finest Ceylon Black Pepper and Vanilla. We combine nature, tradition and modern quality standards to deliver premium products to global markets.

Our Story

Ceylone VanaPep was founded with a passion for Sri Lanka's rich agricultural heritage and a commitment to share it with the world. With our own land and a hands-on approach, we ensure every harvest is carefully nurtured, every batch is responsibly processed, and every shipment reflects the true essence of Sri Lanka.

From our farms to your market – we deliver purity, quality and trust.
